No copyright is claimed over the dictionary data. The data comes without any
warranty whatsoever.

Keeping it current: JMdict is updated continually. On the device, choose
Home -> Update dictionary (u) to download the newest JMdict_e.gz over Wi-Fi
and rebuild the dictionary, or copy a JMdict_e(.gz) file into
tdeckjisho/jmdict/ on the SD card and build from it.
